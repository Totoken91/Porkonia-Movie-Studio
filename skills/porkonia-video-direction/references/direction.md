## Exemple de préparation non testé

Intention : Kevin comprend que le curry menace le serveur. Sur environ 6 s, si cette durée est exposée : il abaisse son téléphone, regarde la fuite, pivote et recule sur sa chaise, puis prononce la prise approuvée avec la bouche visible. Le curry continue de tomber. Ne pas ajouter aussi manipulation du robinet et lever du corps à ce plan court.

Prompt de travail :

“The adult man lowers his phone when he notices curry spilling beside the server, turns his upper body toward the leak, then pushes his chair back a short distance in alarm. The curry keeps dripping into a growing puddle throughout the shot. He hesitates and says the approved French line ‘À… à ce point-là ?’, using the provided performance as the speech reference; keep his mouth unobstructed and visible during the line. Preserve his identity, grey blazer, black shirt and the brewery server-room props from the approved reference while allowing his posture and hands to move naturally. One continuous medium shot with restrained handheld home-video wobble and a small motivated reframe following his reaction. Early-2000s cheap digicam video, imperfect white balance, clipped highlights and sensor noise, 4:3. Continuous quiet server-fan hum and pump rattle underneath speech; wet curry drips and a brief chair scrape matched to the action. No music or subtitles.”

Adapter durée et placement à la prise réelle. Vérifier dans l'image l'espace de recul et la fuite. Ajouter/mixer les sons séparément si nécessaire. Cet exemple ne constitue pas une validation vidéo.
